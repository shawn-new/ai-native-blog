# Against the AI Moratorium: The People's AI Must Be Built
# 反对 AI 暂停键：普通人的 AI 必须被建造出来

## I Agree With The Diagnosis, Not The Pause
## 我同意诊断，不同意暂停

**English:**

I agree with Bernie Sanders's warning about AI power.

Big Tech has every incentive to turn AI into a profit machine: replace labor, suppress wages, expand surveillance, push productivity gains toward capital. Sanders is right that ordinary people should not absorb the costs while billionaires capture the gains.

But I reject his prescription.

Sanders wants a moratorium on new large AI data centers until stronger safeguards exist. That sounds like protection for ordinary people. In practice, it protects the incumbents. Companies that already have compute, cloud platforms, models, capital, and lobbyists can survive scarcity. Later entrants cannot.

That is why this debate matters now. AI infrastructure, regulation, labor politics, and public fear are all colliding in the same window. The rules written here may decide whether AI becomes only a corporate weapon or also a public tool.

Safety has to mean more than slowing down. It has to distribute power.

**中文：**

我同意 Bernie Sanders 对 AI 权力结构的警告。

Big Tech 有强烈激励把 AI 变成利润机器：替代劳动，压低工资，扩大监控，把生产率收益集中到资本手里。Sanders 说普通人不该承担成本、让亿万富翁拿走收益，这个判断是对的。

但我不同意他的处方。

Sanders 想暂停新的大型 AI 数据中心，直到更强的保护到位。听起来像保护普通人，实际可能保护巨头。已经拥有算力、云平台、模型、资本和游说能力的公司，可以熬过稀缺。后来者不行。

这就是为什么现在要讨论。AI 基础设施、监管、劳工政治和公众恐惧正在同一个窗口相撞。这里写下的规则，可能决定 AI 只会成为企业武器，还是也能成为公共工具。

安全不该停在减速上。安全要靠权力分散。

## What Sanders Is Arguing
## Sanders 在说什么

**English:**

Sanders's recent video and policy push make one argument: control of AI is concentrating in the hands of a few tech giants and billionaires.

His point is clear. Without constraints, AI will not naturally serve ordinary workers. It is more likely to replace labor, suppress wages, generate deepfakes, expand surveillance, and consume local electricity and water.

His policy response is the AI Data Center Moratorium Act, introduced with Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez. The bill would pause new large AI data centers until privacy, labor, environmental, electricity, and democratic safeguards are in place.

His diagnosis is strong. His remedy is not.

**中文：**

Sanders 最近的视频和政策动作，说的是同一件事：AI 的控制权正在集中到少数科技巨头和亿万富翁手里。

他的判断很清楚。没有约束，AI 不会自然服务普通工人。它更可能替代劳动、压低工资、制造深伪信息、扩大监控，并消耗社区的电力和水资源。

他的政策回应是和 Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez 一起推动 AI Data Center Moratorium Act。法案要求：在隐私、劳工、环境、电力和民主保护到位之前，暂停新的大型 AI 数据中心建设。

诊断很强。处方不够。

## Point 1: A Pause May Freeze Monopoly
## Point 1: 暂停可能冻结垄断

**English:**

Sanders is worried about concentrated power. That worry is valid. But a pause distributes nothing — it freezes the current map.

**Pause:** Later entrants get blocked first. Giants already have compute, cloud platforms, capital, and lobbying capacity. Scarcer compute raises prices. More complex compliance raises costs. The companies that can absorb those costs are the large ones. Open-source communities, independent developers, and educators who rely on affordable access get squeezed out. The pause Sanders wants would consolidate exactly the power he fears.

**Build:** Early AI diffusion is messy — and that messiness gives small companies, open-source developers, educators, and ordinary workers an opening. Compute, models, tools, and workflows are still moving. Ordinary people can use AI to learn programming, read contracts, analyze financial statements, and build automation. That is already a form of power redistribution.

If the concern is AI monopoly, why choose a policy that pushes scarce resources further toward the companies that already hold them?

Waiting for big companies to become better is not realistic. Big companies are incentive machines. When profit comes from automation layoffs, data extraction, closed ecosystems, and regulatory arbitrage, that is where they go. Without counter-power, capital does not give up capital logic.

Restraint from above is insufficient. Ordinary people also need capability from below.

**中文：**

Sanders 的核心担心是权力集中。这个担心成立。但暂停不会分散权力——它会把现有权力冻住。

**暂停：** 后来者会先被卡住。巨头已经有算力、云平台、资本和游说能力。算力越稀缺，价格越高。合规越复杂，成本越高。最后能承担这些成本的，还是大公司。依赖廉价访问的开源社区、独立开发者和教育者会被挤出。Sanders 想要的暂停，恰恰会巩固他最担心的那种权力。

**建造：** AI 扩散早期很混乱。也正因为混乱，小公司、开源社区、独立开发者、教育者和普通劳动者还有入口。算力、模型、工具和工作流仍在流动。普通人可以用 AI 学编程，读合同，分析财报，搭自动化。这已经是一种权力再分配。

如果担心少数公司垄断 AI，为什么要选一种让稀缺资源更集中在少数公司手里的政策？

等待大公司变好也不现实。大公司是激励机器。只要利润来自自动化裁员、数据攫取、封闭生态和监管套利，它们就会继续往那个方向走。没有对抗力量，资本不会主动放弃资本逻辑。

上面的约束不够。普通人也需要下面的能力。

## Point 2: Ordinary People Must Also Have AI
## Point 2: 普通人也必须拥有 AI

**English:**

In Sanders's story, ordinary people appear in a passive position: workers replaced by AI, voters manipulated by algorithms, data sources harvested by platforms.

All of that is real. But it is only half the story.

**Before:** Understanding a contract required a lawyer. Reading financial statements required accounting training. Automating a workflow required programming. These capabilities were locked behind education, professional networks, and fees most people could not afford.

**After:** An ordinary person can now turn AI into a tutor, opposing counsel, research assistant, coding partner, negotiation coach, and fact checker. The cost of judgment and literacy has dropped.

AI will hallucinate. It will flatter you. It will miss context. It cannot replace judgment — but it can train judgment.

A public that cannot use AI can only be influenced by it. A public that can use AI has a chance to audit it, argue with it, route around it, and organize against it.

**中文：**

Sanders 的叙事里，普通人站在被动位置：被 AI 替代的工人，被算法操纵的选民，被平台收割的数据源。

这些都真实。但只是故事的一半。

**之前：** 想理解合同，要找律师。想看懂财报，要懂会计。想搭自动化，要会编程。这些能力被教育背景、职业网络和大多数人付不起的费用锁住。

**之后：** 一个普通人现在可以把 AI 变成私人导师、反方辩手、研究助理、代码搭档、谈判教练、事实核查员。判断力和见识的获得成本已经下降。

AI 会胡说。会迎合。会漏掉上下文。它不能替代判断——但它可以训练判断。

一个不会使用 AI 的公众，只能被 AI 影响。一个会使用 AI 的公众，才有机会审计 AI、反驳 AI、绕过 AI，并组织起来对抗 AI。

## Point 3: People-First AI Is The Counter-Tool
## Point 3: People-First AI 才是反击工具

**English:**

Big companies will use AI to maximize profit. Ordinary people need AI to sharpen their own bargaining, detection, and organizing power.

People-first AI should not be a softer chatbot. It should be sharper.

A contract reviewer that flags dangerous non-compete terms for workers. A price detector that checks whether a platform is personalizing prices against you. A tenant rights assistant that tells renters whether a landlord's terms are legal. A learning coach that pulls high-quality education out of expensive schools and tutoring systems. A fact-checking tool that traces the holes in political ads, deepfake videos, and corporate PR. An analysis tool for unions that turns scattered complaints into structured evidence.

The goal: make deception harder, exploitation more expensive, information asymmetry smaller.

"AI safety" should mean more than building less AI. The better frame is to build AI that is safety-first, public-first, auditable, and affordable. That still leaves a harder question: if the pause button is the wrong tool, what should regulation actually do?

**中文：**

大公司会用 AI 最大化利润。普通人也需要用 AI 提高谈判能力、识别能力和组织能力。

People-first AI 不该只是更温柔的聊天机器人。它应该更锋利。

合同审阅器，指出竞业协议里的危险条款。价格侦测器，发现平台有没有对你做个性化加价。租客权益助手，帮租户判断房东条款是否合法。学习教练，把高质量教育从昂贵学校和补习体系里释放出来。事实核查工具，追踪政治广告、深伪视频和企业公关稿里的漏洞。工会分析工具，把分散的抱怨变成结构化证据。

目标很清楚：让欺骗更难，让压榨更贵，让信息不对称更小。

"AI 安全"不该只被理解成少建 AI。更好的理解是：建造安全优先、公众优先、可审计、普通人用得起的 AI。这会留下一个更难的问题：如果暂停键用错了，监管到底应该做什么？

## The Counterargument For A Pause
## 支持暂停的最强理由

**English:**

The strongest case for a pause deserves a real hearing.

AI infrastructure is not abstract. Data centers consume electricity, water, land, tax incentives, and grid capacity. If a community bears those costs while gains flow to a handful of platforms, the public has a legitimate objection.

There is also a sequencing argument. Surveillance systems, worker-monitoring tools, deepfake pipelines, and automated replacement workflows — once deployed, the damage may be hard to reverse. A pause buys time for democratic institutions to catch up.

That argument is serious. I just do not think a broad pause achieves it.

The question is whether blocking new infrastructure actually gives power to the public, or gives incumbents more time and scarcity advantage. A good policy should slow harmful uses, tax externalities, force auditability, and fund public capability. A blunt infrastructure pause risks doing only one thing: making AI harder to access for everyone except the companies that already have it.

**中文：**

支持暂停的最强理由，值得认真对待。

AI 基础设施不是抽象东西。数据中心会消耗电力、水、土地、税收优惠和电网容量。如果社区承担这些成本，而收益流向少数平台，公众当然有权反对。

还有一个顺序问题。监控系统、工人管理工具、深伪管线和自动化替代流程，一旦部署，伤害可能很难逆转。暂停可以给民主机构争取追赶时间。

这个论点很严肃。我只是不认为广泛暂停能实现它。

真正的问题是：封堵新基础设施到底会把权力交给公众，还是会给 incumbents 更多时间和稀缺资源优势？好的政策应该放慢有害用途，征收外部性成本，强制可审计，并资助公共能力。钝器式的基础设施暂停只会做一件事：让所有人更难获得 AI，除了那些已经拥有它的公司。

## What Regulation Should Do
## 监管应该做什么

**English:**

Rejecting the pause button does not mean rejecting regulation. Sanders's concerns about electricity, privacy, labor replacement, deepfakes, and democratic manipulation all have real foundations. The direction should be clear: limit harm and expand capacity.

- Charge data centers for real electricity, water, and carbon costs.
- Require independent audits for high-risk AI systems.
- Strengthen data rights and privacy protections.
- Use antitrust policy against vertical bundling across cloud, chips, models, and distribution.
- Give schools, libraries, community colleges, small businesses, unions, and local governments public compute credits and open-source AI tools.
- Require large companies that capture major AI productivity gains to fund worker retraining, profit sharing, and role transitions.
- Support open models, explainable tools, and local deployment.

This path is harder. It is also more serious. The goal is not a slower arrival of AI — it is preventing AI capability from flowing only to capital.

**中文：**

反对暂停键，不代表支持无监管狂奔。Sanders 对电力、隐私、劳动替代、深伪和民主操纵的担忧，都有现实基础。方向应该清楚：管住伤害，扩大能力。

- 对数据中心征收真实的电力、水资源和碳成本。
- 要求高风险 AI 系统接受独立审计。
- 强化数据权利和隐私保护。
- 用反垄断政策限制云、芯片、模型和分发渠道的纵向捆绑。
- 给学校、图书馆、社区学院、小企业、工会和地方政府提供公共算力券和开源 AI 工具。
- 要求因 AI 获得重大生产率收益的大企业承担员工再培训、利润分享和转岗责任。
- 支持开放模型、可解释工具和本地部署。

这条路线更难。也更认真。目标不是让 AI 慢一点到来——而是防止 AI 的能力只流向资本。

## The Safety Test Is Who Gets Power
## 安全测试：权力给谁

**English:**

Sanders's warning is worth hearing. Technology does not automatically side with ordinary people. Capital will turn every new tool into a profit machine until someone stops it.

But pausing construction is not neutral. In a market where giants already lead, capital is concentrated, and infrastructure is scarce, a pause can freeze monopoly. The real question is not whether to regulate AI — it's whether ordinary people end up with any of it.

I choose building. Ordinary people should demand more than a slower arrival of power. They should demand power for themselves.

The test for any AI policy is simple: does this give ordinary people more capability, or less? A policy that passes is democratic. One that fails — no matter how it is framed — is not safety. It is surrender.

Watch the next 18 months: which open-source models survive, whether public compute credits make it into any budget, and whether worker-AI tools scale beyond niche. Those are the real leading indicators. The moratorium debate is the first genuine fight over who AI belongs to — and how it ends will set the frame for everything after.

**中文：**

Sanders 的警告值得听。技术不会自动站在普通人这边。资本会把每一种新工具变成利润机器，直到有人阻止它。

但暂停建设不是中立的。在一个巨头领先、资本集中、基础设施稀缺的市场里，暂停很可能冻结垄断。真正的问题不是要不要监管 AI——而是普通人最终能不能拿到它。

我选择建造。普通人不该只要求权力慢一点到来。普通人应该要求自己也拥有它。

检验任何 AI 政策的标准只有一个：它让普通人的能力变多了，还是变少了？通过这个测试的政策是民主的。通不过的，不管怎么包装，都不是安全——那是放弃。

接下来的 18 个月值得盯紧：哪些开源模型能活下来，公共算力补贴有没有真正进入预算，面向劳动者的 AI 工具能不能跨越小众。这些才是真正的先行指标。暂停争论是第一场真正争夺 AI 归属权的战斗——它如何结束，将定下此后一切的框架。
